## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to define the requirements, goals, scope, and design principles for Medical Tracker, an application designed for healthcare specialists to monitor, manage, and personalize patient care. The platform will enable secure and effective collaboration between patients and health professionals, supported by real-time chat and future AI assistance features.

---

## 2. Scope

Medical Tracker is a digital health platform enabling:

* Health specialists to manage multiple patients, monitor their treatments, and adapt care plans.
* Patients to access their medical history, prescribed treatments, and communicate with specialists.
* AI integration (to be implemented after core functionality) for both specialists and patients to provide smart assistance based on clinical data.

---

## 3. Target Users

### 3.1 Specialists:

* Doctors
* Therapists
* Psychologists
* Nutritionists
* Other certified professionals responsible for managing patient treatments (excluding support roles such as nurses, orderlies, etc.)

### 3.2 Patients:

* Individuals under care of registered specialists

---

## 4. Core Features

### 4.1 User Authentication

* Role-based access (specialist or patient)
* Secure login (email + password)
* JWT-based authentication tokens

### 4.2 Specialist Features

* Add/manage patients (one-directional: only specialists can initiate the relationship)
* View full medical profile of each patient
* Add/edit/delete treatments, medications, and conditions
* View treatment progress and outcomes
* Generate digital prescriptions
* Schedule and manage appointments
* Write consultation summaries and notes
* Real-time secure messaging with patients
* AI Assistant (post-launch): diagnostic aid based on patient data

### 4.3 Patient Features

* View assigned specialists
* Access personal medical data:

  * Conditions
  * Treatments
  * Medications
  * Consultation history
* View/download prescriptions
* Receive instructions and treatment plans
* Real-time secure messaging with assigned specialists
* AI Assistant (post-launch): advice based on personal history and clinical context

### 4.4 Communication

* End-to-end encrypted messaging between patient and specialist
* Notifications for unread messages and appointment changes

### 4.5 Appointments

* Specialist schedules appointments
* Patients can request and confirm appointments
* Statuses: requested, scheduled, confirmed, completed, cancelled

### 4.6 AI Assistant (Post-core phase)

* Accessible only when specialist is unavailable
* Uses historical consultations and diagnoses to suggest advice
* Logs all AI interactions for review

---

## 5. Technical Specifications

### Frontend:

* Framework: **Next.js**
* Styling: **Tailwind CSS**
* Features:

  * Responsive UI
  * Dashboard for both roles
  * Real-time chat UI
  * Appointment calendar

### Backend:

* Framework: **Python (FastAPI)**
* API-first design with JWT authentication
* Modular endpoints for users, treatments, messages, etc.

### Database:

* **MongoDB**
* Collections: users, patients, appointments, prescriptions, messages, consultations, instructions, ai\_logs
* Indexed fields: email, user role, timestamps, patient-specialist relationships

---

## 6. Compliance & Legal

* Follows **GDPR** and **LOPDGDD** (Spain):

  * Explicit consent for data handling
  * Role-based access control
  * Data encryption
  * Patient right to access, modify or delete data
* All health data stored in **EU-based** servers

---

## 7. Flexibility & Future Scope

* Modular system architecture to allow new features
* Easily extendable to mobile apps using same backend APIs
* Future roles (e.g., lab technicians, admin staff)
* Analytics and reporting modules
* AI upgrades (predictive models, NLP summaries)

---

## 9. Non-Functional Requirements

* Performance: Fast responses (<300ms average per API call)
* Security: HTTPS-only, rate limiting, audit logging
* Availability: 99.9% uptime
* Scalability: Horizontal scaling of backend and DB

---

## 10. Assumptions

* Users will have access to smartphones or computers
* Internet connection is required
* Only verified professionals may register as specialists
* AI functionality will be integrated **after** all other modules are complete
